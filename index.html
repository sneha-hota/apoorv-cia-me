    REVENUE FALLS

    Price vs demand – interactive simulator

    This is an illustrative model, not reported data. Defaults are calibrated to real anchors: Carrols (largest BK franchisee) Q4 2021 – average order +12.1% with traffic −4.2% (implied ≈ −0.35); Revenue Management Solutions found traffic fell sharply once price rises passed about 10–13%. Real prices: Whopper averaged $4.19 (Dec 2019) vs $7.79 (2024) per one analysis (+86%); a BK meal rose from $6.76 to $8.18 (+21%) in 2021–22 (MoneyGeek). Burger prices industry-wide rose about 14% from Jan 2023 (Datassential) while beef costs rose about 32%.

    Key insights

    • RBI revenue more than doubled from $4.05B (2015) to $9.43B (2025); the only fall was COVID-hit 2020 (−11.3%), followed by +15.5% in 2021.
    • Burger King runs roughly 19,900 restaurants in 120+ markets, about 95% franchised, so growth is driven by openings abroad as much as US sales.
    • International is the engine: BK international comparable sales +4.8% in 2025 vs +1.6% at BK US (US system sales only +0.8% to $11.6B).
    • China is the big growth bet: about 1,250 restaurants today with a joint venture targeting 4,000+ by 2035.
    • Pricing is a trade-off: beef inflation (+20% in 2025) squeezed US franchisee profits, so value offers and price tipping points matter.
